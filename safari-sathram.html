Safari Overview

Sathram is one of Idukki's most breathtaking hill viewpoints, located roughly 24 km from Kumily near Vandiperiyar. It historically served as a resting outpost for traditional pilgrims trekking to Sabarimala.

The 4x4 trail winds through vintage British-era tea plantations, mist-covered mountain switchbacks, and dramatic ridge tracks overlooking the sheer drop into Tamil Nadu. This route is exceptionally famous for frequent sightings of massive Indian Gaur (Bison) herds and wild mountain deer feeding in the open meadows.
