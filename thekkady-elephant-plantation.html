Thekkady Elephant Packages & Spice Safaris

Regular Rides, Photoshoots, Traditional Dressup & Full-Day Premium Circuits

Elephant rides in Thekkady, Kerala, offer a unique way to experience the lush forests and wildlife-rich landscapes of the area. Home to the legendary Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, Thekkady is famous for its elephant experiences, where visitors can ride through spice plantations and forested paths on these majestic animals, guided by experienced mahouts (elephant caretakers). These elephant rides typically take you on a gentle trail through the green surroundings, offering a chance to see the region’s diverse flora and sometimes glimpse other wildlife. Choose from our complete range of official packages—from quick rides and trunk shower baths to pre-wedding photoshoots and all-inclusive full-day premium adventures!

Ethical & Responsible Elephant Experiences

While Thekkady is a prime spot for elephant activities, the experiences are typically respectful of the animals and often emphasize conservation efforts. Operators follow ethical practices under the oversight of animal welfare guidelines, ensuring regular health check-ups, natural river bathing, and rotation schedules. Guests not only enjoy rides but also learn how mahouts care for, communicate with, and bond with these gentle giants.
