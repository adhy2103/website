Overview

The Gavi Eco-Tourism Safari is one of the most authentic and immersive wilderness programs in the Periyar Tiger Reserve region. Operated under KFDC (Kerala Forest Development Corporation), this full-day eco-tourism experience offers a perfect combination of forest exploration, wildlife observation, trekking, boating, plantation visits, and guided activities inside a protected reserve.

Covering nearly 80 km round trip from Kumily, the route passes through highways, deep reserve forests, cardamom plantations, and wildlife-rich corridors—making it one of the best day-long adventure programs in Thekkady.

Distance & Route Breakdown

Total Distance: Approx. 80 km

Kumily to Gavi: 40 km one-way (80 km full round trip)

20 km Highway Route

Smooth paved drive from Kumily through scenic high-range settlements to Vallakkadavu checkpost.

20 km Deep Reserve Forest Road

Restricted, unpaved deep forest trail with canopy tunnels, crystal streams, and the highest probability of spotting wild elephants, gaurs, and rare fauna.

Start Time: 5:30 AM Departure

  • 🌅 Prime Wildlife Activity: Animals (elephants, gaurs, deer) are most active in the early morning hours near waterholes and grazing clearings.
  • 📋 Forest Entry Clearance: Arriving early at Vallakkadavu checkpost guarantees smooth verification and entry before the daily quota fills.
  • 🌿 KFDC Schedule Alignment: The official KFDC morning eco-program and hot breakfast begin right as you reach the Gavi Centre.
  • 🌫️ Optimal Climate & Visibility: Cool mountain breeze, rolling mist, and clear golden-hour lighting for nature photography.

Gavi Safari Highlights

Reserve Forest Drive

The 20 km reserve forest journey is rich in flora, fauna, natural streams, and deep forest canopy landscapes.

Boating at Gavi Reservoir

A serene, guided boating session inside the tranquil Gavi lake surrounded by mist-covered evergreen trees.

Skeleton Museum

An educational stop where visitors can view preserved wildlife skeletons collected historically from the reserve.

Cardamom Plantation Visit

Explore authentic natural cardamom fields, learn organic cultivation techniques, and witness processing kilns.

Guided Trekking (Short / Long)

Choose between a relaxed short nature trail or an immersive long forest trek, both led by trained eco-tourism staff.

Wildlife Sightings

Frequent sightings of Asian Elephants, Indian Bison (Gaurs), Deer, Nilgiri Langurs, Sambar, Wild Boars, birds & butterflies.
