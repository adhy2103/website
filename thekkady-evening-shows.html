Thekkady’s Famous Evening Entertainment

After an exhilarating morning jeep safari through the rugged Western Ghats or relaxing afternoon spice garden walk, Kumily’s evening shows provide the quintessential Kerala cultural experience. All theaters are centrally located within 5–10 minutes of major hotels and resorts in Thekkady, making it easy to watch one or multiple shows back-to-back.

Pro-Traveler Tip: Evening shows have strictly limited seating and frequently sell out during weekends and holiday seasons. Advance WhatsApp seat reservation ensures front-tier row placement and skips ticket-counter queues!

The 4 Evening Shows You Can Experience

Choose individual tickets or bundle your preferred shows with combo savings.

Daily 6 PM & 7 PM • 1 Hour
1. Kalaripayattu Martial Arts

Kalaripayattu is one of the oldest martial arts in the world, with deep cultural significance in Kerala. It includes intense physical movements, weapon training, and defensive techniques. Performances take place in special arenas called Kalari, where skilled practitioners demonstrate agility, strength, weapon sparring, and precision—climaxing in daring jumps through flaming rings of fire.

₹300 – ₹400 / Person
Daily 5 PM & 7 PM • 1 Hour
2. Kathakali Classical Dance-Drama

Kathakali is a world-renowned classical dance-drama known for its elaborate costumes, vibrant face makeup, and expressive storytelling through facial expressions (*Navarasas*) and hand gestures (*Mudras*). Performers use natural mineral pigments to portray characters from Hindu epics like the Mahabharata and Ramayana, accompanied by live vocalists and thunderous Chenda drum rhythms.

₹300 – ₹400 / Person
Daily 7 PM & 8 PM • 45 Mins
3. Musical Water & Laser Symphony

A mesmerizing open-air night show where computer-controlled water fountains shoot up to 40 feet high, dancing gracefully in synchronization with rich cinematic sound systems, multi-colored lasers, and mist video projections portraying Kerala's history and mythology under the stars.

₹250 – ₹350 / Person
Daily 6 PM – 7 PM • 1 Hour
4. Live Magic & Illusion Show

An enchanting live stage illusion show tailored for families, couples, and children. Master illusionists perform close-up card miracles, telekinesis, escapology, and comedic audience interaction inside a state-of-the-art air-conditioned auditorium in Kumily.

₹250 – ₹350 / Person
