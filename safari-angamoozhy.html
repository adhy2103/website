The Extreme Off-Road Experience

The Angamoozhy route is engineered for those who want an authentic, unpaved off-road expedition through the interior valleys of Pathanamthitta and Idukki. Unlike standard tarmac sightseeing, this circuit puts high-torque 4x4 systems to work across slippery mud ruts, rocky riverbeds, and dense bamboo forests.

Driven by our expert drivers who know every stone and ditch on this route, you will experience heart-pumping river stream crossings where water splashes around the tires, visits to remote tribal settlements, and raw nature without crowded tourist infrastructure.
