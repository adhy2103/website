Indian Gaur (Wild Bison)

Massive, muscular highland bovines with distinctive pale stockings. Often seen grazing peacefully in herds along high-altitude cliff meadows of Sathram.

Driver Tracking Tip: 85%+ sighting rate along the Sathram border ridge during early morning dew and afternoon golden hour.

Kariyili & Malayanman

Nilgiri Langur & Giant Squirrel

Glossy black primates with golden-brown manes and vibrant rainbow-hued Malabar Giant Squirrels leaping across 100-foot evergreen tree canopies.

Driver Tracking Tip: Listen for distinctive barking alarm calls and heavy canopy rustling during mid-morning forest drives.
Kerala State Bird

Great Indian Hornbill & Birds

Magnificent prehistoric-looking birds with giant golden-yellow casques, gliding silently over deep jungle valleys alongside emerald doves and trogons.

Driver Tracking Tip: Frequent flyers around fruiting wild fig trees along the Gavi & Angamoozhy forest roads from Nov to May.

Everything You Need To Know

Direct answers from our Kumily operations desk regarding permits, child safety, seasonal timings & booking policies

The Kerala Forest Development Corporation (KFDC) strictly limits entry to approximately 30 private vehicles per day into Gavi's protected core zone to safeguard wildlife corridors. Permits sell out days or weeks in advance during peak season (Oct–May) and weekends. We strongly recommend reserving your slot at least 3 to 7 days prior via our Kumily desk so we can secure your vehicle pass, driver entry, and official meal vouchers without last-minute disappointment.
Gavi eco-tourism has two components:
1. Private 4x4 Jeep Tariff (₹3,500/jeep): Dedicated vehicle, experienced mountain driver, fuel, and hotel pickup/drop in Kumily.
2. KFDC Eco-Tourism Pass (₹2,065/person): Official forest entry permit, reservoir row-boating, guided forest museum walk, hot buffet breakfast, and authentic Kerala vegetarian buffet lunch at the forest canteen.
Note: Mandatory Forest Entry Pass is ₹45 for Indian Nationals and ₹500 for Foreign Nationals per head.

Gavi Insider Chronicle

A Day in the Deep Woods

Hour-by-hour breakdown of what your Gavi Eco-Tourism Safari experience looks like — from pre-dawn Kumily departure to golden-hour return

05:30 AM

🌄 Pre-Dawn Departure from Kumily

Your private 4x4 Jeep picks you up at your hotel entrance in the dark cool mist. Light jackets are essential. The road climbs out of town as the sky turns from indigo to deep violet — no other tourists in sight yet.

06:15 AM

🛂 KFDC Forest Checkpost — Permit Verification

Entry at the official Gavi Forest Gate. Our team pre-registers your permits so there's zero delay. Rangers stamp the vehicle logbook. Beyond this point, private civilian cars are strictly forbidden — only licensed KFDC operators enter.

06:30 – 08:30 AM

🐘 Peak Wildlife Window — 80 km Deep Forest Drive

This is the golden hour. Elephant herds graze by the waterways, Indian Gaur bulls patrol ridge clearings, and Nilgiri Langurs watch from canopy branches. Your driver navigates silently — engine off at key wildlife corridors.

08:30 AM

☕ Hot Breakfast — Gavi Eco-Tourism Camp

A hearty Kerala breakfast is served at the official KFDC camp: hot idly, sambar, coconut chutney and tea/coffee. Eat with the morning mist rising from the reservoir valley — no restaurants or cafés exist in this forest zone.

09:00 – 11:00 AM

🚣 Gavi Reservoir Boating + Guided Nature Trek

Paddle across the serene Gavi reservoir by boat, watching Painted Storks and Indian Cormorants. Then walk the guided jungle trail with a forest naturalist — spotting spice trees, rare orchids and animal tracks in the undergrowth.

11:30 AM

🍛 Kerala Lunch — Forest Kitchen

A full Kerala-style meals lunch is included in your KFDC package — rice, sambar, rasam, mixed vegetable curries, pickle and payasam. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian options available. Freshly cooked on forest firewood.

12:00 – 02:00 PM

🏛️ Gavi Forest Museum + Cardamom Estates

Explore the Kerala Forest Department's interpretive museum — flora and fauna exhibits, tiger conservation records, tribal heritage displays. Then walk through working cardamom and pepper estates with your driver as guide.

02:00 – 04:00 PM

🐾 Return Drive — Evening Wildlife Pass

The return journey often yields second wildlife sightings as animals emerge again from midday rest. Your driver knows alternate forest routes for elephant observation. Stop freely for photography — your jeep, your pace.

04:30 PM

🏠 Return to Kumily — Hotel Drop

Drop-off at your hotel or a preferred point in Kumily. Full debrief with your driver on wildlife sightings and photos. Evening free for Periyar Lake boat cruise or spice market shopping.
