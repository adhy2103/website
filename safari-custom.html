VIP Private Safari Service

Ideal for honeymooners, photography enthusiasts, birdwatchers, and families wanting complete privacy and zero rush. You have exclusive hire of a top-condition 4x4 vehicle with a courteous, English & Hindi-speaking local driver.

You decide when to start (as early as 04:30 AM for misty sunrise peaks or a relaxed 09:30 AM), which spots to spend extra time at, where to stop for lunch or drone shots, and when to wrap up.

Sunrise Mountain Peaks

Catch morning cloud oceans at Ottakathalamettu, Sathram or Ramakkalmedu.

Secret Waterfalls & Streams
